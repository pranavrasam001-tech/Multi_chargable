# Multi-Device Desktop Charging Station

A 3D CAD project designed using **Autodesk Fusion 360**. The idea behind this project is to create a single desktop charging station where multiple devices can be placed in an organized way instead of keeping separate chargers and cables on the desk.

Printables :- https://www.printables.com/model/1920749-multi_chargable_station

## About the Project

This charging station is designed to hold a smartphone, smartwatch and wireless earbuds in one place. The design includes separate sections for each device, along with space for charging components and cable connections.

I worked on the overall shape, enclosure, device holders and internal layout to make the station look neat and practical. The main focus was on creating a compact design that keeps the devices properly arranged and makes better use of desk space.

## Main Features

- Separate inclined holder for a smartphone
- Circular charging dock for a smartwatch
- Dedicated compartment for wireless earbuds
- Space for USB-C and USB-A ports
- Internal space for PCB reference models and cable arrangement
- Enclosure with a removable bottom cover
- Blue and white colour combination for the final design

## Tools Used

- Autodesk Fusion 360 – 3D modelling, assembly and rendering
- External component references – for planning the charging component layout

## Design Process

The project was completed in different stages:

1. **Initial Layout:** Planned the overall shape and decided where each device would be placed.
2. **Main Enclosure:** Created the base body, top platform and bottom cover.
3. **Device Holders:** Designed the smartphone stand and smartwatch dock.
4. **Internal Layout:** Added the earbuds compartment and planned the placement of charging components and cable connections.
5. **Final Assembly:** Adjusted the alignment of the parts, refined the design and prepared the final render.

## Assembly Instructions

follow these steps to put together the multi-device desktop charging station

1. **prepare the parts:** collect all the 3d-printed parts and check that they are clean and ready for assembly.

2. **place the internal components:** fit the charging boards and other required components into their designated spaces inside the main enclosure.

3. **attach the smartphone holder:** position the phone holder on the upper platform and secure it using the planned mounting arrangement.

4. **fit the smartwatch holder:** place the smartwatch charging dock in its designated position and make sure it is properly aligned.

5. **install the earbuds compartment:** fit the earbuds compartment into its allocated space on the upper platform.

6. **manage the cables:** arrange the cables through the provided routing space to keep them organised and prevent them from getting trapped inside the enclosure.

7. **close the enclosure:** fit the bottom cover and check that all the components are properly positioned.

8. **check the final assembly:** inspect the overall fit, alignment and appearance of the charging station to make sure all parts are in place.
